Backyard Chickens for Beginners: The First 8 Weeks

Most beginner problems with backyard chickens are decided before the birds arrive. This is the order that works.

Before they arrive

  • Coop: 2-3 sq ft per bird inside, 8-10 sq ft in the run
  • Ventilation above head height, open all winter
  • Check local rules - many areas allow hens but not roosters

Weeks 1-2: settle in

Keep them in the coop and run for the first week so they learn where home is. Expect a drop in laying while they settle.

Weeks 3-4: routine

Feed layers pellets at roughly 1/4 lb per bird per day, grit on the side, clean water. Collect eggs daily.

Weeks 5-8: the first real problems

Pecking, mites and muddy runs all show up here. Space and dryness prevent most of it - crowding causes the rest.
