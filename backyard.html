A Self-Sufficient Backyard: Where to Start

Self-sufficiency on a normal-sized lot is less about growing everything and more about wasting nothing. Start with these three.

1. One real food bed

Not six beds you cannot keep up with - one bed, deep enough to hold moisture, close enough to the house that you actually water it.

2. A compost pile that works

Roughly equal parts greens and browns, turned occasionally. A cubic metre is the smallest size that heats up properly.

3. Somewhere to store food

The harvest arrives faster than you can eat it. Plan shelves, jars or a cool corner before the glut, not after.

What to add later

Rainwater, chickens, fruit trees - all of them are easier once the first three are running.
