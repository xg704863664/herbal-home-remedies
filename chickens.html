Backyard Chickens for Beginners: What to Set Up First

Most first-year chicken keeping mistakes are set up before the birds arrive. These are the things worth getting right first.

1. Space per bird

Plan on roughly 2-3 square feet inside the coop per bird, and 8-10 square feet in the run. Crowding is the single biggest cause of pecking problems.

2. Bedding and ventilation

Ventilation matters more than warmth. A coop that is sealed up against the cold will hold ammonia and moisture, which causes respiratory problems. Leave high vents open all winter.

3. Feed and the real cost

A laying hen eats roughly 1/4 pound of feed a day. Add bedding, grit and the occasional vet bill and most backyard flocks run $20-40 per bird per year - worth costing out before you buy.

4. Local rules

Check your city or HOA rules before you build: many allow hens but not roosters, and some set minimum distances from neighbouring houses.
