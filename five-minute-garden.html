Growing Food in 5 Minutes a Day

Most small food gardens fail from neglect, not from lack of skill. A five-minute daily routine prevents that.

Daily: water and look

Water in the morning, at the base, and check the undersides of leaves. Most pest problems are visible a week before they become a problem.

Weekly: the two jobs that matter

Feed once a week and sow a small pinch of something new. Succession sowing - a little, often - is what keeps a small bed producing instead of giving you one giant harvest in August.

What to grow in a small space

Salad leaves, herbs, radishes and climbing beans give the most food per square foot. Sweetcorn and pumpkins do not.
