Herbal Remedies for Sleep: What to Try First

Most sleep herbs work gently and slowly. The ones below are the traditional starting points - useful, cheap, and easy to grow or buy.

Chamomile

The best known and the mildest. One heaped teaspoon of dried flowers per cup, covered, steeped 5-10 minutes, 30-45 minutes before bed.

Lemon balm

Gentler than valerian and easier to take every night. Good if anxiety is what keeps you awake rather than pain.

Valerian root

The strongest of the common options, and the one most likely to leave you groggy. Start with a low dose and do not combine with alcohol.

Passionflower

Often paired with valerian. Traditionally used where the problem is falling asleep rather than staying asleep.

What matters more than the herb

Same bedtime, dark room, no screens for the last hour. Herbs help a good routine; they do not replace one.
