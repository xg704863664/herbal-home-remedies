Herbal Remedies for Coughs: What Actually Helps

Most cough remedies sold in shops do very little. The herbs below are the ones traditionally used for coughs and colds - as teas, syrups and steam inhalations.

  • Thyme - traditionally used as a tea for coughs, and the base of the old-fashioned cough syrup.
  • Marshmallow root - soothing for a dry, tickly cough; usually taken as a cold infusion.
  • Liquorice root - a classic for sore throats. Not for people with high blood pressure.
  • Elderberry - popular through cold season, usually as a syrup.
  • Peppermint - helps with the blocked nose that comes with a cold.

How to make a simple thyme tea

Pour boiling water over a teaspoon of dried thyme, cover, steep 10 minutes, strain. Honey helps - but never give honey to children under one.
