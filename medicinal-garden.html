Medicinal Garden Layout: What to Plant Where

A medicinal garden does not need much space - it needs the right herbs in the right spots. Here is how to lay one out.

Start with the sunniest spot

Most medicinal herbs want 6+ hours of sun. Thyme, sage, rosemary and oregano all belong in the brightest corner, and they prefer poorer, drier soil than vegetables.

Give the spreaders their own space

Mint, lemon balm and chamomile spread aggressively. Either give them a bed of their own or sink them in pots up to the rim.

Group by harvest, not by looks

Put the herbs you cut often - parsley, chives, mint - closest to the path. The ones you harvest once or twice a season can go at the back.

Leave room to dry

Plan where you will dry and store the harvest before you plant. A drying rack needs more space than most people expect.
