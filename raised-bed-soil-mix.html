Raised Bed Soil Mix: A Simple Recipe That Works

Filling a raised bed is where most of the cost is. These ratios keep it cheap and still grow good food.

How much soil you actually need

A 4x8 ft bed at 12 inches deep takes roughly 32 cubic feet - about one cubic yard. At 6 inches deep it halves. Most beginners over-buy.

The basic mix

  • 60% topsoil
  • 30% compost
  • 10% coarse sand or aged manure for drainage and feed

Fill the bottom first

Logs, branches, cardboard and turf at the bottom third cuts the volume you have to buy. It settles over the season - top up in spring.

Do not use pure compost

It dries out, shrinks and burns young roots. Compost is an ingredient, not the whole mix.

Common questions

How deep should a raised bed be?

25-30cm (10-12in) suits salad, herbs, onions and most roots. Go to 40cm for carrots and maincrop potatoes.

Can I fill a raised bed with just compost?

No. Pure compost shrinks, dries out and can burn roots. Mix roughly 60% topsoil with 30% compost.

How much soil for a 4x8 raised bed?

About 32 cubic feet at 12 inches deep, or roughly one cubic yard. Filling the bottom third with logs or branches reduces that.
