What to Feed Backyard Chickens (and How Much)

Feeding chickens is simple until you start reading forums. The short version: layers pellets, grit, clean water, and a few rules about scraps.

How much per day

Roughly 1/4 lb (110-120g) of layers pellets per bird per day. Free-fed from a hopper is fine; they self-regulate.

What layers pellets contain

Balanced protein (16-18%), calcium for shells, and the vitamins they would otherwise find foraging. Changing brands suddenly can stop laying for a few days - mix the old and new for a week.

Grit and oyster shell

Grit helps them digest anything fibrous. Oyster shell, offered separately, is for hens that need extra calcium for strong shells.

Scraps: what to avoid

  • Anything mouldy
  • Avocado, raw potato and green potato skins
  • Salty or oily leftovers
  • Citrus in quantity

Common questions

How much feed does a laying hen eat per day?

About 1/4 lb (110-120g) of layers pellets per bird per day.

Do backyard chickens need grit?

Yes. Grit helps them break down fibrous food. Offer oyster shell separately for calcium.

Can chickens eat kitchen scraps?

In moderation. Avoid mouldy food, avocado, raw or green potato, salty or oily leftovers.
